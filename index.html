Outguessing the AI Bubble — Interactive Simulator

A financing bubble is forming in the leveraged tail of AI infrastructure — not a technology bubble. This calculator re-runs the article's three Monte Carlo simulations in your browser, so you can change the inputs and see how wide the range of honest outcomes is. As of November 8, 2026. Every number below is a model estimate, not a fact and not a forecast.

Research and education — not investment advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. The article's own verdict: a bubble is forming in the financing layer; a pop is not yet proven. Audited OpenAI/Anthropic financials do not exist publicly; no hyperscaler discloses GPU utilisation; exact Oracle contract terms are not in any public filing.

The tail's median is fine; its downside is a cliff. Diversification barely changes the median — and nearly eliminates the catastrophic branch.

What this simulator cannot do. The simulations inherit every weakness of their inputs. MC-1 blends two anchors that measure different things. MC-2/MC-3 distributions are assumptions, not measurements. The three simulations are not independent — a bad prospectus moves all three at once, so their probabilities cannot simply be multiplied. A Monte Carlo cannot discover a risk nobody put into the model — the power constraint appears nowhere in these numbers.
